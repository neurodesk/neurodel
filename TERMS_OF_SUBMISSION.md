# Terms of Submission — NeurodeskEDU

*Last updated: May 2026*

Thank you for contributing to NeurodeskEDU! By submitting a pull request to this repository you agree to the following terms.

---

## 1. Publication and Review Are Independent

When you open a pull request, a GitHub Action automatically validates that your notebook runs completely from top to bottom without errors. Once that check passes and the pull request is merged, your notebook is published immediately. The peer review process then runs **in parallel** and does not gate or delay publication.

## 2. A Peer Review Will Be Initiated

After your notebook is published, a peer review will be opened automatically in the [neurodeskedu-reviews](https://github.com/neurodesk/neurodeskedu-reviews) repository. The review assesses scientific quality, reproducibility, and conformance with [NeurodeskEDU design principles](https://github.com/neurodesk/neurodeskedu#design-principles).

You are welcome to suggest one or more reviewers when you open your pull request — for example, a colleague with relevant domain expertise, or someone who has reviewed similar notebooks before. If you do not suggest anyone, the Neurodesk team will identify and invite suitable reviewers on your behalf.

## 3. A Review Badge Will Appear on Your Notebook

Your notebook will display a badge reflecting the current review status (e.g. *Under Review*, *Reviewed*). The badge updates automatically as the review progresses and provides readers with transparent quality information.

## 4. You Are Expected to Engage With Reviewers

Once a review is opened, reviewers may raise questions, flag issues, or request changes. You are expected to respond to reviewer comments **within a reasonable timeframe** (typically 15 days). Constructive engagement keeps the collection healthy and benefits the community. If you anticipate a delay, please say so in the review thread.

## 5. Neurodesk Retains the Right to Maintain Your Notebook

To keep NeurodeskEDU notebooks functional and consistent over time, the Neurodesk team reserves the right to make maintenance edits to your notebook, including:

- updating deprecated function calls or API changes in the underlying tools;
- adjusting layout, metadata, or formatting to conform to current NeurodeskEDU requirements;
- fixing broken data links or environment references so the notebook continues to execute correctly.

Such edits will be made transparently via pull request or commit, and you will be notified where possible.

## 6. Unresponsive Submissions May Be Superseded

If a reviewer raises substantive concerns and there is **no response from the contributor within a reasonable period** (typically 30 days after follow-up), Neurodesk reserves the right to:

- create a new notebook that implements the reviewers' suggestions; and
- cite your original notebook as the direct starting point and give appropriate attribution.

This is a last resort intended to preserve the quality and usability of the collection, not to diminish your contribution.

## Summary

| What happens | When |
|---|---|
| Notebook published | After GitHub Action passes and PR is merged |
| Review opened in neurodeskedu-reviews | Shortly after merge |
| Review badge appears on notebook | Automatically |
| Contributor expected to respond to reviewers | Within ~15 days |
| Neurodesk may make maintenance edits | Ongoing |
| Neurodesk may create a successor notebook | If no response after ~30 days of follow-up |
